FAQ

Is ImageShrink free?

A free edition is available from GitHub. A full version with expanded batch capabilities is available on itch.io.

Does ImageShrink upload my images?

No. Image processing is performed locally on your computer.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes. ImageShrink is specifically designed for batch image resizing and compression.

Can I resize images to stay below a file-size limit?

ImageShrink includes a target file-size option for workflows where images need to fit under an upload or delivery limit.

What operating system does it support?

ImageShrink is designed for Windows.
